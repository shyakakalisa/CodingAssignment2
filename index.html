A Fresh and Simple Kale Salad

Kale Salad

Recipe by: Shyaka Kalisa

Photo of a fresh kale salad.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups chopped kale
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup sliced cucumber
  • 1/4 cup shredded carrots
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Equipment

  • Large mixing bowl
  • Cutting board
  • Knife
  • Measuring spoons

Directions

  1. Wash and dry the kale.
  2. Remove the tough stems and chop the kale into small pieces.
  3. Place the kale in a large mixing bowl.
  4. Add the olive oil and lemon juice.
  5. Gently massage the kale with clean hands until it begins to soften.
  6. Add the tomatoes, cucumber, and carrots.
  7. Season with salt and pepper and mix well.
  8. Serve and enjoy.
